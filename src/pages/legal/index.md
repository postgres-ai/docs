# Legal Documents

PostgresAI is committed to transparency and compliance with data protection regulations. Below you'll find our legal documents and policies.

## Documents

### [Terms of Service](/tos/)
Our Terms of Service govern your use of PostgresAI products and services.

### [Privacy Policy](/privacy/)
Our Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

### [Data Processing Addendum (DPA)](/legal/dpa/)
Our DPA sets forth the terms for processing personal data under GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection laws.

:::tip How to Execute the DPA

The DPA published here is for **review purposes**. To make it legally binding:

1. **Review** the DPA terms on this page
2. **Email** legal@postgres.ai with subject: `DPA Execution Request - [Your Company]`
3. **Include** your contact name, title, and company address
4. **Receive** a DocuSign envelope within 5 business days
5. **Execute** both parties sign → DPA is now legally binding

:::
